Old manuscripts used to have [colophons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colophon_(publishing)) with information about their construction, such as the place, calligrapher, illustrator, and date. Books still often have this: the copyright page usually details the publisher and the book's edition. Sometimes the typeface is listed too.

## This website's colophon

This website is generated by [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) + [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) and hosted on Github Pages. You can read its [source code](https://github.com/cgm616/cgm616.me). The primary typeface for body and display text is the variable font [Literata](https://www.type-together.com/literata-font) designed by TypeTogether. Some elements are set in [Public Sans](https://public-sans.digital.gov/) designed by the General Services Administration of the US government.
